# Deleting your TradeGuard account

## In app (immediate)
Open **Settings -> Delete Account** inside the TradeGuard app and confirm. 

This permanently removes:
- Your profile and authentication account
- Broker connections and MetaAPI credentials
- Journal trades, notes, and uploaded screenshots
- Guard history and equity snapshots
- Copy-trading settings and signal follows

## By email
If you cannot access the app, email support@tradeguard.app from your registered email address. After verification, we will complete the deletion within 30 days.

## What is NOT deleted
- **Active Subscriptions:** Deleting your account does *not* cancel an active TradeGuard Pro subscription. You must cancel it manually through your Apple App Store or Google Play Store subscription settings to stop billing.
- **Analytics Data:** Aggregated or anonymized crash statistics and analytics.

## Before you delete
Deletion is irreversible. Please contact support@tradeguard.app first if you would like an export of your trading journal before your account is removed.
